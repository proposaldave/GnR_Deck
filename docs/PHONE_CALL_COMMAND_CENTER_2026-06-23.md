# Phone Call Command Center

Purpose: make the phone-call strategy operational enough that Dave can prove the NEPC growth loop and the Give n Receive product thesis in the same week.

## DECISION

The next operating system is not a better blast. It is a call-driven next-action machine.

The job of the machine:

- choose the next person to call
- give Dave the exact opener
- ask the first high-signal question
- listen for demand, hesitation, tone, social fit, and referral paths
- create one next action
- update the next call queue

## Why This Is The Right Wedge

Middleton membership growth depends on more than price. It depends on whether the club can turn lower-level, connection-first players into regulars, members, referrers, lesson buyers, clinic demand, and event participants.

A phone call is the highest-signal version of that loop because it reveals:

- what the person wants
- what they are confused about
- when they can play
- who they would enjoy playing with
- whether they feel known by the club
- whether they can bring someone else
- whether membership is a value problem, an awareness problem, or an experience problem

## Call Queues

### Queue 0: Quo Deliverability

Do not start the warm call sprint while the number is still showing as spam risk.

Next action: keep pushing Quo support until the business number, caller profile, carrier registration, display name, and reputation status are clean enough for legitimate customer follow-up.

### Queue 1: Beginner Recovery

Call recent beginner/top-of-funnel players tied to beginner events, Quick Start-style pathways, or low-level social play.

Objective:

- fill the immediate beginner event if still live
- learn why email did not move them
- identify the next right beginner offer
- surface membership confusion
- find friends they might bring

First question:

> I am calling because I want to understand what would actually make NEPC easy and fun for you. Are you mainly looking to play more, learn more, meet people, bring friends, or figure out whether membership makes sense?

### Queue 2: Middleton Member Growth Sprint

Call 20 Middleton members to test referral awareness, referral incentive strength, member-hosted private parties, glow/event interest, and whether they know people who should become members.

Objective:

- defend price with evidence
- identify private-party hosts
- identify membership candidates
- learn whether the current referral reward is invisible

First question:

> We are looking at a few ways to make it easier for members to bring friends into Middleton. Did you know we currently have a membership referral bonus?

### Queue 3: Lesson Demand

Call or text people who have already signaled lesson demand.

Objective:

- capture desired lesson length
- capture availability
- identify preferred pro/location
- identify whether they also want clinics/classes
- prevent demand from disappearing

First question:

> I know you were interested in a lesson. Do you usually prefer 60 minutes, 75 minutes, 90 minutes, or whatever gets you on the court fastest?

### Queue 4: Happy Follower / Referral Hubs

Call people who have had a strong Dave-led event experience.

Objective:

- learn what made the event work
- ask when they want more
- ask who should be invited next
- form new groups from existing connection

First question:

> You seemed to have a great time in that group. What made it work, and who else would you want to bring into that kind of experience?

## Live Next-Question Rules

If the person sounds excited:

- ask for one concrete next action now
- ask for one intro text
- ask what day/time would make it easy

If the person sounds confused:

- ask what they think membership includes
- ask what they wish someone had explained
- avoid pitching until the confusion is clear

If the person sounds hesitant:

- ask what would make the experience feel low-pressure
- offer a beginner/social/on-ramp path
- ask whether bringing a friend would help

If the person names a friend:

- ask for a text intro while the call is warm
- do not settle for "I'll think about it"

If the person wants lessons/classes:

- collect desired length/topic/availability
- do not offer unverified times
- create a demand card for Dave/staff review

## Scorecard

Track this daily:

- calls attempted
- live conversations
- useful transcripts
- one-next-actions created
- events filled
- private lessons scheduled
- lesson/class/clinic demand cards
- referrals named
- intro texts requested
- membership conversations started
- membership confusion found
- private-party prospects
- glow/event prospects
- script changes from transcript evidence

The business KPIs that matter most:

- net Middleton memberships sold
- membership conversations started
- conversion from beginner/top-of-funnel call to paid next action
- court utilization in targeted weak slots
- event fill rate
- instructor hours booked
- revenue per available court hour
- referrals named and intro texts sent
- first-30-day repeat participation for new/lower-level players

## Proof Standard

The proof is not that Dave made a lot of calls.

The proof is:

> Cold email produced little or no movement. Human calls produced demand, scheduling, referrals, membership conversations, and a better next-call protocol. AI turned the calls into a repeatable next-action machine.
