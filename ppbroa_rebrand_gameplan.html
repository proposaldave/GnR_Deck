THE REAL PROBLEM
GnR

The old club model waits for the wrong customer.

PPBROA should stop optimizing for the narrow peak-hour player and go after the huge beginner and recreational base already playing at the YMCA and public outdoor courts.

Current trap

High-friction membership, empty off-peak inventory, manual programming, and no software layer that manufactures the next right group.

New target

Warm bodies in the building first. Skill growth, social fit, and paid peak play second. The mass base makes the economics possible.

THE WEDGE
GnR

Win by becoming the obvious place to play.

600 members is the first operating target, because density is what makes matchmaking, events, and utilization work.

Strategy

Make the membership fee low enough that recreational players join before they overthink it. Then use programming and matchmaking to make PPBROA feel easier, warmer, and more alive than public courts.
